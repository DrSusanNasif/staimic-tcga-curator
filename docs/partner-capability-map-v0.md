# Partner capability map v0

## Purpose
Map potential partners who could support STaiMIC TCGA Curator as a reproducible cancer-data curation and training project.

## Partner categories

### Academic oncology and bioinformatics groups
Role: scientific validation, methods review, student training, cancer genomics expertise.

### Hospitals and molecular tumor boards
Role: clinical relevance, real-world interpretation needs, translational oncology use cases.

### AI and health-tech startups
Role: AI-readiness, data engineering, model development, decision-support prototypes.

### Cloud and infrastructure partners
Role: scalable computing, secure data workflows, reproducibility, deployment support.

### Cancer foundations and patient organizations
Role: visibility, patient-centered framing, advocacy, fundraising links.

### Public institutions and innovation bodies
Role: regional development, funding alignment, education, policy support.

## Evaluation criteria
- scientific relevance
- data expertise
- clinical connection
- funding potential
- training value
- ease of first contact

## First action
List 5 priority partners and classify each using the criteria above.
